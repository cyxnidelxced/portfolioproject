User Experience Collaborative Team Project

Project Overview

This project is created to show focus on user experience by designing a mobile app that is purposed for a target audience in the mental health field. This is a collaborative effort between team members who are working to study UX Design.

My Role

Designer and Researcher

Timeline

March 2025 - May 2025

The Solution

The User Experience project explores the concepts of UX Design for digital products. By executing user-centered design practices, our team was able to create a mobile app that could be put on the market with further study.

  • Conducted research in preparation for designing experience and interaction design deliverables
  • Critiqued interactive interfaces to determine both positive and negative design patterns
  • Planned and conducted simple user testing sessions to evaluate the effectiveness of tested interface designs
  • Designed an app through wireframing, prototyping, and trial and error
